Story so far

Running narrative. Add a paragraph per session so a new reader can catch up in two minutes.

After Session 0

The party sailed across the ocean and landed on the shore of a continent none of them had seen before, where the forests dwarf anything back home. A Warden of Thornhaven met them at the dock and marched them through the strange, sweet-smelling, light-filled forest to the city itself: a network of stone buildings and druid-grown bridges wound around the trunks of enormous Star Bark trees. They climbed from the shadowy Rootway at the base, through the smoky workshops of the Boughs, up to the Canopy, where Soren recognized the Star Bark trees as the magical heart of the whole region.

In the grand hall they walked across a pool of magical water that holds weight like glass. A rival party, the Dawn Striders, was already there, and their long-haired bard leader picked a fight with Jotham almost immediately. The city's head druid (green-haired, older, name not yet learned) explained that Thornhaven's wardens have been disappearing, that earlier adventurers who came to help have also vanished, and that the two parties would each be given a task to prove themselves. When the bard tripped and fell through the "solid" water (with some unseen help from the party), the druid decided Jotham looked like the more capable of the two leaders.

Jotham chose the road-clearing task over the wolf task, so the Dawn Striders took the wolves. Solara, Master of Trade, rushed the party into a wagon and explained that traders and citizens have gone missing along the trade routes, that the wardens are stretched thin, and that she will watch from a distance rather than help directly. At the forest road they found a fallen tree blocking the main southern route, a bridge to the east, a path to the west, and an overpowering stench of rot. Durian set the fallen tree alight, and out of the brush came undead wolves. Erlathon was bitten for 18 damage in the first exchange, Soren and Jotham were dragged into the woods by their cloaks, and something much larger stalked the treeline. Jotham smote one wolf into ash with radiant energy, Soren wild shaped into a bear to break free, and the session ended mid-fight with four wolves and one very large one still standing.

The party

Jotham
Paladin. Drow heritage. Party leader.

Front-line melee with a longsword and shield. Divine Smite (radiant), Shield of Faith. Has a past that let him read the Rootway as thief territory at a glance. Quick with a sarcastic remark, and quick to get into a banter fight with anyone who condescends to him.

Session 0: Volunteered as leader. Sarcastically named the party "the Pool Walkers." Chose the road task after checking with the group. Dragged into the woods by an undead wolf, then killed it in one round with longsword plus Divine Smite (18 damage, radiant disintegrated it). Cast Shield of Faith on himself. Said he has two spell slots left for smites.
Soren
Druid 3, Circle of the Moon.

Recognized the Star Bark trees from druid lore. Can wild shape into a brown bear (CR 1, multiattack). Foundry sheet currently caps wild shape at CR 1/4, which the DM is fixing; brown bear stats need to be ready before the next session.

Session 0: Nature check identified the Star Bark trees. Grappled and pulled into the brush by a wolf. Wild shaped into a brown bear, rolled a 20 to escape, landed one bear attack and missed the other. Ended the round back to back with Erlathon.
Aurelian
Rogue 3 (has the Shield spell, so likely Arcane Trickster). Possibly a halfling (someone rerolled with Lucky).

Scout and skirmisher. Bow and shortsword. Cunning Action. The DM noted that Aurelian's player "knows this campaign a little," so Aurelian may have useful meta knowledge. Tends to go off alone; DM flagged this as risky for this season.

Session 0: First onto the magical water. Stealth 20 scouting ahead, but the wolves' passive perception beat it. Wolf attack on him missed (Shield not needed). Dropped bow, hit a wolf with shortsword for 6, disengaged with Cunning Action, then spotted the biggest wolf he has ever seen right beside him.
Erlathon
Ranger 3. 35 ft speed (likely wood elf).

Archer. Hunter's Mark, Entangle. Best nose in the party: first to smell the undead. Knows ranger hand signals.

Session 0: Smelled the rot and signaled the party. Bitten by an undead wolf for 18 in the opening round; passed a Constitution save against something from the bite. "Badly injured" and one hit from dropping. Cast Entangle on the big wolf and the one next to him; both saved, but the area is now difficult terrain. Moved away and the opportunity attack missed.
Durian
Bard 3.

Caster and trickster. Known spells or cantrips seen: Mage Hand, Elementalism, Command, Tasha's Hideous Laughter. Arcana checks.

Session 0: Read the magical energy in the hall (Arcana 15: the tiefling in the Dawn Striders is strong). Used Mage Hand to tie the rival bard's shoelaces together in front of the head druid. Set fire to the fallen tree with Elementalism, which flushed out the wolves. Cast Command ("drop") on the wolf holding Jotham; it saved. Planning Tasha's Hideous Laughter next.
Party gaps identified in Session 0
  • Nobody has healing potions. Everyone used theirs in the Season 1 finale and nobody restocked before boarding the ship.
  • The party does not know each other's spells and abilities. Agreed to compare kits so they can plan as "a unified brain."
  • Only Jotham has radiant damage, which is what actually kills the undead wolves. Everyone else needs to protect him and set up his smites.

City guard and forest protectors. Uniformed.

One met the party at the dock and escorted them to the city, warning them about the Rootway. Others lined the grand hall. They are the ones going missing, and they are stretched thin investigating "other occurrences."

Rival party: the Dawn Striders

Locations and lore

The Emerald Canopy

The forest region around Thornhaven. Redwood-scale trees but greener and lusher. Smells of pine and maple with a sweetness in the air, not damp. Light reaches the forest floor where a normal canopy would block it. Described as "almost ethereal." Nothing like the forests on the party's home continent.

Star Bark trees

The famed trees Thornhaven is built into. Inherently magical; their energy is what makes the Emerald Canopy what it is. They grow only here. Every attempt to plant Star Bark seeds elsewhere in the world has failed. Soren could feel their power by touching one.

Thornhaven

City built into and around the Star Bark trees, mixing druidic living bridges with elven stone architecture. Home for eons to the Druids of the Verdant Path. Once at war with the elves of the Verdant Spire to the north; the two made peace and the city is the product of both peoples living in harmony. Three levels:

  • The Rootway (bottom): dark, light barely reaches it. Home to the poorer and "more lively" citizens. Thieves, crooks, and unsavory types. The Warden warned: don't linger alone, watch your pockets. Jotham recognized the vibe from his own past.
  • The Boughs (middle): reached by spiraling wooden staircases around the trunks. Workshops, smoke, the clang of armor and weapons being forged, flashes of magic from enchanting shops. Likely the place to buy gear and potions.
  • The Canopy (top): the same magical energy felt on entering the forest. Site of the grand hall.
The Grand Hall and the Pool

A majestic pool of magical water fills the hall. Walking on it feels like solid glass or a mirror, though water visibly moves beneath. It can apparently let someone through when it wants to (the Dawn Striders' bard went in with a splash), and water beads off like Gore-Tex and rejoins the surface without a ripple. An overhanging platform to the north is where the council addresses visitors. Bright light pours in from above, enough to hide a Mage Hand in.

The Southern Road

Main trade road out of Thornhaven. At a clearing the main road continues south, a road splits east over a bridge, and a path goes west. A fallen tree blocks the southern road. The tree is too large to vault. Durian set part of it on fire. Undead wolves lair in the brush around it, and the rot smell is strongest to the south.

The Verdant Spire

Elven realm to the north of Thornhaven. Formerly at war with the druids, now at peace. Not yet visited.

Lessons and tactics

Things the DM said out loud or that the fight made obvious.

  • Radiant kills the undead. Jotham's smites are the party's win condition. Anyone with radiant or similar options should look for them as the season goes on. Set Jotham up; do not scatter.
  • Fight as a team. The DM's stated design goal. "There's no more 'I'm getting this one, I'm getting this one.' Use your strengths and protect your weaknesses."
  • Do not split off. Aurelian's solo scouting got him boxed in next to the biggest threat. Stealth 20 was not enough against these wolves.
  • Prepare before travel. The DM asked what the party wanted to do before boarding; nobody restocked and now there are no potions. Buy supplies before every expedition.
  • Share kits. Nobody knew who had what. Compare spell lists before Session 1.
  • Rules reminders from the DM (2024 rules, played stricter this season):
    • Ranged attack with an enemy within 5 ft is at disadvantage.
    • Hiding in combat is an action, so you cannot Disengage (via Cunning Action) and Hide in the same turn without a second bonus action.
    • Moving away without Disengage provokes an opportunity attack. Disengage is an action (Cunning Action makes it a bonus action for rogues).
    • If you cast a leveled spell as your action, your bonus action spell that turn must be a cantrip.
    • Entangle: save is only on cast. Failures are restrained; successes still leave the 20 ft square as difficult terrain. Half in the cube counts.
    • Shield of Faith must target yourself explicitly in Foundry or it lands on the enemy.
  • Slots are scarce. Jotham has two smites left and Erlathon needs healing from somewhere. Spend big resources on the huge wolf, not the strays.

Session 0 Thursday, September 10, 2026. Ended 10:45 pm mid-combat.

Summary

Arrival in the Emerald Canopy, tour of Thornhaven, audience with the council, choice of proving task, and the opening round of a fight with undead wolves on the southern road. Session ran long on Foundry setup issues after the VTT update; the DM called it a "getting back into the swing of things" session.

Minutes

  1. Landfall. The party sights the impossibly green forest from a couple of miles out at sea. A Warden meets them at the shore, confirms they are the ones who answered the call, and hurries them toward Thornhaven.
  2. The forest. Long march through the Emerald Canopy. Pine and maple smell with a sweetness, light where there should be shade. Warden explains Thornhaven's history: Druids of the Verdant Path, old war with the elves of the Verdant Spire, now harmony.
  3. The Rootway. Dark lower level. Aurelian's passive perception and Jotham's criminal instincts both read it as thief territory. Warden: don't linger alone, watch your pockets.
  4. The Boughs. Spiral staircases up the trunks to the workshop level. Smoke, forging, enchantment magic.
  5. The Canopy. Soren rolls Nature and identifies the Star Bark trees: magical, unique to this place, cannot be grown anywhere else.
  6. Foundry pause. DM restarts to fix the token action HUD mod, which is incompatible with the new Foundry version. Not resolved; players use character sheets directly.
  7. The grand hall. A pool of magical water. Another party stands on its surface. Aurelian steps out first and finds it solid as glass. Jotham asks the rival group what the point of the pool is; their long-haired bard calls him dense and asks if he is a "drow bull," then walks off. Erlathon confirms the surface continues. Durian's Arcana (15) reads overwhelming magic in the hall and strong energy from the rival tiefling. Guards in warden garb line the perimeter.
  8. The council. Jotham asks a guard who is in charge and is pointed to the platform. The green-haired head druid arrives in a swirl of leaves, greets Soren by name ("I see you, and welcome"), and lays out the situation: wardens missing, earlier adventurers missing, outsiders invited at Solara's urging. She asks where the party hails from; Aurelian answers "the other side of the ocean." Aurelian says they hunger for battle and points to their scars; the rival bard jokes about raccoons.
  9. Leaders and names. The bard declares himself leader of the Dawn Striders. Jotham steps up as leader of the party. Asked for a name, the party has none. Out-of-character brainstorm produces: the Unbound, the Darkness of Lathander, Durick's Doom, Pool Walkers, Fandalin's Heroes, the Fandalin Five, Woodland Wanderers, Deathmark, Greyborne, the Tribe of Fandalin, and the Trojan Horses. DM defers the decision to a Discord vote. In character, Jotham tells the druid "the Pool Walkers."
  10. The shoelaces. As both leaders are called forward, Durian uses Mage Hand (Arcana check, hidden in the bright light) to tie the bard's shoelaces. The bard face-plants through the water and comes up soaked, baffled at how he got wet. Durian whispers "it's magical water." The druid rolls her eyes and tells Jotham he is probably the more capable of the two.
  11. The two tasks. Task one: clear a disrupted main road (Solara briefs). Task two: wolves attacking caravans to the north, behaving strangely (Tharlik briefs). Jotham chooses the road, checking with the group first; Soren backs him and adds that he would rather not hurt animals. The bard sneers that they took the easy one and leaves, after retying his shoe.
  12. Solara. Head druid vanishes. Solara, Master of Trade, jumps down and briefs at speed while walking. Wagon ride out. Traders and citizens missing on the routes, wardens stretched thin, she will watch but not help, and she will say more once they prove themselves. Jotham asks how to contact her (no clear answer) and what the reward is (no answer). Aurelian asks for advice and gets "It's your job to figure it out," then winks at her.
  13. The clearing. Main road south with a fallen tree across it, road east over a bridge, path west. Aurelian stealths ahead (20) behind trees, sees nothing. Erlathon's Perception picks up a foul smell to the south and he signals with ranger hand gestures. Jotham listens, hears nothing useful. Aurelian argues for going east across the bridge to approach the tree from behind; Jotham is skeptical. Durian moves closer and casts Elementalism to put fire on the fallen tree.
  14. Ambush. The stench resolves into an undead wolf bursting from the brush. Initiative. Aurelian is allowed to count as hidden from his earlier Stealth, but the wolf's passive perception beats it. The wolves roll very high.
  15. Round 1, wolves. Wolf bites Erlathon: Constitution save (passed), 18 damage, "badly injured." A second wolf grabs Soren from behind and drags him into the woods (failed Dexterity save). A third does the same to Jotham (failed Dexterity save, DC 12). A fourth lunges at Aurelian and misses.
  16. Round 1, Durian. Casts Command ("drop") on the wolf holding Jotham's cloak. It saves and just stares at him.
  17. Round 1, Aurelian. Drops bow (adjacent enemy would mean disadvantage), hits the wolf with shortsword for 6, Cunning Action Disengage, moves toward the tree. Cannot hide as well, cannot vault the tree this turn. Perception check: the stench gets stronger and the biggest wolf he has ever seen is right next to him.
  18. Round 1, Soren. Wild shapes into a brown bear (Foundry needed manual override). Strength check with advantage, rolls a 20, escapes the grapple. Multiattack on the wolf: one hit, one miss. Wolf still up. Moves back to back with Erlathon.
  19. Round 1, Jotham. Longsword hit on the wolf holding him, Divine Smite for 2d8 radiant, 18 total. Kills it: the sword cuts, then the radiant energy disintegrates the wolf. Narrated as kicking it off his cloak and driving the glowing blade down its throat. Bonus action Shield of Faith on himself (+2 AC).
  20. Round 1, Erlathon. Rules discussion on move, action, and bonus action. Casts Entangle as an action on the big wolf and the wolf beside him. Both pass the Strength save; area becomes difficult terrain. Moves 35 ft away toward the dead wolf. Opportunity attack from his wolf misses (narrated as the vines slowing it). Party realizes nobody has potions.
  21. Session end. 10:45 pm. DM: the party is "in a pickle," radiant damage is the answer, work as a team, Solara is available if needed. Next date TBD, after the wedding on September 25.
