Season 2 Campaign Journal

Thornhaven and the Emerald Canopy. 2024 rules. Running record, updated after every session.

Story so far

Running narrative. Add a paragraph per session so a new reader can catch up in two minutes.

After Session 0

The party sailed across the ocean and landed on the shore of a continent none of them had seen before, where the forests dwarf anything back home. A Warden of Thornhaven met them at the dock and marched them through the strange, sweet-smelling, light-filled forest to the city itself: a network of stone buildings and druid-grown bridges wound around the trunks of enormous Star Bark trees. They climbed from the shadowy Rootway at the base, through the smoky workshops of the Boughs, up to the Canopy, where Soren recognized the Star Bark trees as the magical heart of the whole region.

In the grand hall they walked across a pool of magical water that holds weight like glass. A rival party, the Dawn Striders, was already there, and their long-haired bard leader picked a fight with Jotham almost immediately. The city's head druid (green-haired, older, name not yet learned) explained that Thornhaven's wardens have been disappearing, that earlier adventurers who came to help have also vanished, and that the two parties would each be given a task to prove themselves. When the bard tripped and fell through the "solid" water (with some unseen help from the party), the druid decided Jotham looked like the more capable of the two leaders.

Jotham chose the road-clearing task over the wolf task, so the Dawn Striders took the wolves. Solara, Master of Trade, rushed the party into a wagon and explained that traders and citizens have gone missing along the trade routes, that the wardens are stretched thin, and that she will watch from a distance rather than help directly. At the forest road they found a fallen tree blocking the main southern route, a bridge to the east, a path to the west, and an overpowering stench of rot. Therion set the fallen tree alight, and out of the brush came undead wolves. Erlathon was bitten for 18 damage in the first exchange, Soren and Jotham were dragged into the woods by their cloaks, and something much larger stalked the treeline. Jotham smote one wolf into ash with radiant energy, Soren wild shaped into a bear to break free, and the session ended mid-fight with four wolves and one very large one still standing.

After Session 1

The fight on the southern road went on for three more rounds, and the party learned how hard these wolves are to put down. Soren, still a bear, soaked up bite after bite and choked on their stench. Spells kept bouncing off: Therion's Sorcerous Burst sailed straight through a rotted hole in one wolf, and the huge one, now clearly an undead dire wolf, shrugged off Aurelian's Tasha's Hideous Laughter. Jotham ran out of smites and had to be handed a spell slot back to land one more. Just as the party was flagging, Solara came running in with a spectral wolf spirit at her side and an old friend at her heels: Masato, the party's straw-hatted warlock from Season 1, back without a word of explanation. Solara put an arrow through one wolf's skull and her spirit tore down another, Masato blasted a third, and the dire wolf slunk into the brush to wait.

Aurelian baited it out with a Mage Hand from the top of the fallen tree and put a readied arrow into its chest as it leapt at him, though it still knocked him flat. The party piled on, Solara patched up Erlathon, and Masato finished it with a Scorching Ray that the table narrated as a red sky and a beam of fire that burned the dire wolf to nothing. Five undead wolves lay dead. Looking at the tree, the party found that it had not fallen on its own: the base was rotted through by necrotic magic while the rest of the trunk was healthy. They hauled it off the road, and the proving task was done.

Solara pointed them to a river to the east to rest. Beside a waterfall they found a lone traveler from a city called Ironford, cheerfully camped and apparently unaware of any danger, who said he was waiting for friends. His surprise seemed genuine, but Jotham did not like the feel of him, so the party camped on their own along the riverbank while Solara kept watch at the bridge. After the long rest Solara headed back to Thornhaven to report the road clear and asked the party to keep digging into who rotted the tree. The party decided to go back to the city first to report and finally buy potions. As they turned to leave, the traveler's camp across the water was no longer one person. It was three.

The party

Jotham
Paladin 3, Oath of Vengeance. Drow. Party leader.

Front-line melee with a longsword and shield. Divine Smite (radiant), Shield of Faith. Has a past that let him read the Rootway as thief territory at a glance. Quick with a sarcastic remark, and quick to get into a banter fight with anyone who condescends to him. Working on his temper.

Session 0: Stepped up as leader when the council asked and nobody else would. Sarcastically named the party "the Pool Walkers." Chose the road task after checking with the group. Dragged into the woods by an undead wolf, then killed it in one round with longsword plus Divine Smite (18 damage, radiant disintegrated it). Cast Shield of Faith on himself. Said he has two spell slots left for smites.
Session 1: Found he had no slots left: Foundry had logged Shield of Faith twice. The DM gave one slot back, and on a missed longsword swing let him smite anyway for 18 radiant ("the damage happened before the blade even touched"). Out of slots after that; missed twice more with the longsword, then landed a plain hit on the dire wolf. Thought about his Drow Faerie Fire but had already used his action. Pushed hard for a long rest before investigating anything. Did not trust the lone traveler by the river ("I just don't get a good vibe") and talked the party into camping on their own. Thanked Masato for showing up in time.
Soren
Druid 3, Circle of the Moon. Halfling.

Driven to study the feeling of connection to the moon as different animals. Recognized the Star Bark trees from druid lore. Wild shapes into a brown bear (CR 1, multiattack, 40 ft speed, keen smell). The DM set the bear up in Foundry by hand in Session 0 and it worked all of Session 1. Cantrips include Starry Wisp and Mage Hand.

Session 0: Nature check identified the Star Bark trees. Used Mage Hand to tie the rival bard's shoelaces together in front of the head druid. Grappled and pulled into the brush by a wolf. Wild shaped into a brown bear, rolled a 20 to escape, landed one bear attack and missed the other. Ended the round back to back with Erlathon.
Session 1: Spent the whole session as the bear. Took a 12-point bite with pack advantage, then failed a Constitution save against the wolves' stench (his bear nose made it worse) for another 1d4. Knocked a wolf prone with his multiattack and mauled it until it was barely holding together. Roared to draw the pack onto himself, which worked: he took 16 more. Hit the dire wolf with Starry Wisp and charged in to roar in its face over the downed Aurelian. Sensed necrotic magic in the rotted base of the fallen tree. Smelled a campfire to the east that led the party to the river. Stood up on his hind legs and waved at the terrified traveler. Still working out how to turn back into a halfling at the end of the night.
Aurelian
Rogue 3, Arcane Trickster. Possibly a halfling (someone rerolled with Lucky); unconfirmed.

Scout and skirmisher. Shortbow and shortsword. Cunning Action, Sneak Attack. Spells seen: Shield, Mage Hand, Tasha's Hideous Laughter. The DM noted that Aurelian's player "knows this campaign a little," so Aurelian may have useful meta knowledge. Tends to go off alone; DM flagged this as risky for this season.

Session 0: First onto the magical water. Stealth 20 scouting ahead, but the wolves' passive perception beat it. Wolf attack on him missed (Shield not needed). Dropped bow, hit a wolf with shortsword for 6, disengaged with Cunning Action, then spotted the biggest wolf he has ever seen right beside him.
Session 1: Cast Tasha's Hideous Laughter on the dire wolf; it saved. Dashed past the burning tree, then threw up Shield (AC 15 to 20) just in time, which the DM ruled also blocked the stench. Tried to read the dire wolf with Nature and found it was not acting like a normal dire wolf. Hit it with the shortbow for 8, then scrambled on top of the fallen tree on the second Athletics try for the high ground. Perception 25 with advantage: smelled it within 10 feet but could not see it. Sent a Mage Hand into the brush as bait and readied a shot; when the dire wolf leapt, he hit it for 11 with Sneak Attack, then lost the Dexterity tie and was knocked flat. Investigated the tree. Flirted with Solara and lost ("Maybe I'll see you on your back someday." "How rude."). Approached the traveler with his bow drawn, asked the questions, read his surprise as genuine, and patted him down: nothing.
Erlathon
Ranger 3, Fey Wanderer (Dreadful Strikes). 35 ft speed (likely wood elf).

Archer with a longbow and the Archery fighting style (+2 to attack, already counted on the sheet). Hunter's Mark (Favored Enemy: two free casts per long rest), Entangle, Hail of Thorns, Ensnaring Strike. Dreadful Strikes adds 1d4 to weapon hits, not spells. Best nose in the party: first to smell the undead. Knows ranger hand signals.

Session 0: Smelled the rot and signaled the party. Bitten by an undead wolf for 18 in the opening round; passed a Constitution save against something from the bite. "Badly injured" and one hit from dropping. Cast Entangle on the big wolf and the one next to him; both saved, but the area is now difficult terrain. Moved away and the opportunity attack missed.
Session 1: Marked a wolf with Hunter's Mark and hit it with the longbow for 16 (Hunter's Mark plus Dreadful Strikes). When the dire wolf vanished into the brush, cast Hail of Thorns over the spot it was hiding in for 3 and got a snarl back. Healed for 13 by Solara's Cure Wounds. Asked Solara for advice and was pointed to the vendors in the Boughs. Felt the forest's uneasiness lift once the wolves were dead. Backed Jotham's call to camp apart from the stranger: "We should protect our own."
Therion
Sorcerer 3, Draconic.

Caster and trickster. Go-to spell: Sorcerous Burst, usually just called "SB." Also seen: Mage Hand, Elementalism, Command, Tasha's Hideous Laughter. Arcana checks.

Session 0: Read the magical energy in the hall (Arcana 15: the tiefling in the Dawn Striders is strong). Set fire to the fallen tree with Elementalism, which flushed out the wolves. Cast Command ("drop") on the wolf holding Jotham; it saved. Planning Tasha's Hideous Laughter next.
Session 1: Sorcerous Burst on a wolf missed, narrated as the blast going straight through a rotted hole in its body. Next round, SB'd the wolf mauling Soren and left it on its last legs (the table tried to argue the damage type was "radiant lightning"; it was not). Could not find the hidden dire wolf, took the advice not to waste resources, and parked himself next to Solara. The DM walked him through the Ready action for next time. Helped, barely, to haul the tree off the road.
Masato
Warlock 3, likely the Fiend patron (Scorching Ray is on the Fiend list). Straw hat and robe. Back from Season 1.

A familiar face from Season 1, back in the party with no explanation of where he has been, and nobody asked. Carries a blade. Eldritch Blast (120 ft) is his workhorse; Scorching Ray is the big one, three separate fire rays, each its own attack roll.

Session 1: Came running in behind Solara mid-fight and rolled into initiative. Nearly blasted Solara's spirit wolf before the DM pointed out it was on his side. Killed an undead wolf with Eldritch Blast, then finished the dire wolf with Scorching Ray. Made a crack at Solara and got a stank look and "no healing for you." Ran the longest way of anyone to reach the fight and was too winded to help much with the tree. "The good old days, huh?" Player had to leave right at the end of the session.
Party gaps, as of Session 1
  • Still no healing potions. Everyone used theirs in the Season 1 finale and nobody restocked. The party has decided to go back to Thornhaven and buy them in the Boughs before going further.
  • Jotham runs dry fast. Three 1st-level slots cover smites and Shield of Faith, and after that he is swinging a plain longsword.
  • Radiant is still the best answer to the undead, and Jotham still has most of it. Masato's fire (Scorching Ray) turned out to work too.
  • Nobody in the party has much healing. Solara's Cure Wounds was the only real healing all fight.

Main thread Season arc

The Vanishing Wardens Active
Given by: the head druid of Thornhaven. Session 0.

Wardens who have protected Thornhaven for ages are disappearing. Thornhaven put out a call for adventurers; those who answered have also gone missing. The council extended the invitation across the ocean to find a stronger party. The head druid was reluctant to invite outsiders and did so at Solara's urging. Solara says she will brief the party further once they have proven themselves.

Status: Not yet directly assigned. The proving task is now done, so the party is due a proper briefing when they report back. Solara's "we didn't lose another group" suggests other parties have died on these roads.

Proving tasks Session 0

Clear the Southern Road Done
Given by: Solara, Master of Trade. Chosen by Jotham.

A main trade road through the forest has been disrupted. Investigate and clear it. Traders and even citizens have gone missing along these routes. Solara is not assisting but is monitoring from a distance; the party can reach out if they need help (how, exactly, was not answered).

Session 0: A fallen tree blocks the road south, guarded by undead wolves. Combat ongoing at the end of the session.
Session 1: All five undead wolves dead (two of them by Solara). The party hauled the tree to the side of the path. The DM confirmed that moving the tree was the whole task, so it is complete. Still to do: report back in Thornhaven.
Who Rotted the Tree? Active
Given by: Solara. Session 1.

The tree across the southern road did not fall on its own. It was broken on purpose by unnatural means: the base is rotted and necrotic in one small spot while the rest of the trunk is healthy. Soren sensed necrotic magic there. Solara does not believe mindless creatures did this, and asked the party to investigate what caused the blockage.

Status: Open. Leads: the necromancy that raised the wolves, the smaller trail branching east off the southern road, and the traveler's camp by the river.
The Northern Wolves Taken by the Dawn Striders
Given by: Tharlik (council member). Not chosen.

Trade routes to the north are being hit by wolves attacking caravans. The forest is normally in harmony with its wolves, but these are "acting strange." Given what the party is now fighting on the southern road, these are almost certainly the same undead wolves.

Status: Rival party has it. Worth tracking whether they come back.

NPCs and factions

The Council of Thornhaven

Each council member has a different role in protecting the city.

The Head Druid
Name unknown. Green hair, older, wrinkled. Elder of the council.

Arrived in a rush of leaves and wind; everyone in the hall deferred to her. Protecting the forest is her first duty. Skeptical of outsiders, invited the party only because Solara pushed for it. Greeted Soren personally ("I see you, and welcome"), which may mean she recognizes a fellow druid or knows something about him. Not amused by Jotham's sarcasm but sized him up as the more capable leader.

Solara
Master of Trade. Half-elf. Quest giver for the road task. Archer and caster.

Brisk, walks fast, briefs on the move. Slightly taller than Erlathon. Refused to give tips: "It's your job to figure it out." Says she will monitor the party and can be reached for help, and will brief them further on the bigger problem if they prove capable.

In Session 1 she proved she really was watching. She ran into the fight with Masato behind her, summoned a smaller, ethereal spirit wolf to fight beside her, killed one wolf with an arrow through the skull, and let her spirit kill another. Healed Erlathon for 13 with Cure Wounds and another party member for 7 afterward. "Not that I don't think you can handle yourselves, but I saw a lot of commotion and I needed to make sure we didn't lose another group." Dry with Aurelian ("decades of practice; seems like you could use some"), cold to Masato after his crack ("no healing for you"). Does not need to rest; kept watch at the bridge overnight, then went back to Thornhaven to report the road clear.

Tharlik
Council member. Briefs the wolf task.

Only named, not met. The Dawn Striders went to him. A guard may have also given his name (heard as "Darlake Einbach") when Jotham asked who was in charge; transcription is unclear.

On the road

The traveler from Ironford
Lone adventurer camped by the river east of the southern road. Session 1. Name not asked.

Friendly and cheerful ("Ahoy, ahoy, fellow travelers"). Says he is from Ironford, heading up to Thornhaven because he has always heard about the city's beauty, and was waiting to meet some friends because the path north was blocked. Had no idea there was any danger. Aurelian's Insight read his surprise as genuine, a pat-down found nothing, and he shivered at the word "undead" and offered to share his camp. Soren's sniff turned up nothing. Jotham did not trust him and the party camped elsewhere. When they left after their long rest, his camp had three people in it.

The Wardens

Wardens of Thornhaven
City guard and forest protectors. Uniformed.

One met the party at the dock and escorted them to the city, warning them about the Rootway. Others lined the grand hall. They are the ones going missing, and they are stretched thin investigating "other occurrences."

Rival party: the Dawn Striders

The Dawn Striders
Rival adventuring party. "Named because we stride into the dawn after victorious battle."
  • The Bard (leader): long hair, arrogant, condescending. Called Jotham "dense," mocked his scars ("probably got those from a little raccoon"), and called the road task the easy one. Fell face first into the magical pool after Soren's Mage Hand tied his shoelaces. Therion whispered "it's magical water." Grudge established.
  • The Tiefling: woman with horns, radiates strong magical energy (Therion's Arcana). The table's consensus was that she is very attractive.
  • The Elf: silent, straight faced, stayed in the back.
  • At least one more member (two laughed at the bard's joke).
Status: Took the northern wolves task. Whether they survive it is an open question.

Locations and lore

The Emerald Canopy

The forest region around Thornhaven. Redwood-scale trees but greener and lusher. Smells of pine and maple with a sweetness in the air, not damp. Light reaches the forest floor where a normal canopy would block it. Described as "almost ethereal." Nothing like the forests on the party's home continent.

Star Bark trees

The famed trees Thornhaven is built into. Inherently magical; their energy is what makes the Emerald Canopy what it is. They grow only here. Every attempt to plant Star Bark seeds elsewhere in the world has failed. Soren could feel their power by touching one.

Thornhaven

City built into and around the Star Bark trees, mixing druidic living bridges with elven stone architecture. Home for eons to the Druids of the Verdant Path. Once at war with the elves of the Verdant Spire to the north; the two made peace and the city is the product of both peoples living in harmony. Three levels:

  • The Rootway (bottom): dark, light barely reaches it. Home to the poorer and "more lively" citizens. Thieves, crooks, and unsavory types. The Warden warned: don't linger alone, watch your pockets. Jotham recognized the vibe from his own past.
  • The Boughs (middle): reached by spiraling wooden staircases around the trunks. Workshops, smoke, the clang of armor and weapons being forged, flashes of magic from enchanting shops. Solara's recommendation for gear and potions: "ample supply and many different vendors."
  • The Canopy (top): the same magical energy felt on entering the forest. Site of the grand hall.
The Grand Hall and the Pool

A majestic pool of magical water fills the hall. Walking on it feels like solid glass or a mirror, though water visibly moves beneath. It can apparently let someone through when it wants to (the Dawn Striders' bard went in with a splash), and water beads off like Gore-Tex and rejoins the surface without a ripple. An overhanging platform to the north is where the council addresses visitors. Bright light pours in from above, enough to hide a Mage Hand in.

The Southern Road

Main trade road out of Thornhaven. At a clearing the main road continues south, a road splits east over a bridge, and a path goes west. The fallen tree (about six feet thick, too big to vault, climbable with an Athletics check) was rotted through at the base by necrotic magic; the rest of it is healthy apart from Therion's scorch marks. After Session 1 it lies along the edge of the path and the road is open. Past it the road continues south, and a smaller trail branches off to the east. The forest's uneasiness lifted once the wolves were dead.

The river and the waterfall

East of the southern road. A river with a bridge across it and one of the most beautiful waterfalls Jotham has ever seen. The traveler from Ironford camped at the water's edge. The party long rested on the bank away from the bridge, so the water covered one side, while Solara watched the bridge.

Ironford

A city the traveler by the river says he comes from. The party has never heard of it. Location unknown.

The Verdant Spire

Elven realm to the north of Thornhaven. Formerly at war with the druids, now at peace. Not yet visited.

The trade routes

With the southern road blocked, merchants who used to come up from the south have been going around and entering Thornhaven from the north instead (per Solara, Session 1). That is the same northern side where the Dawn Striders went to deal with wolves attacking caravans.

Undead wolves
Southern road, Sessions 0 and 1. A pack of 4 regular plus 1 dire wolf.

Wolves with flesh sloughing off; one's jaw hung partly detached but still bit. Their bodies have necrotic holes rotted through them, big enough for a spell to pass through without touching anything. Smell of rot carries far ahead of them. Bites hit hard (18 on Erlathon, 12 and 16 on bear Soren) and they get advantage when a packmate is close. They grapple and drag targets into the brush by the cloak (Dexterity save, DC around 12). Passive perception high enough to spot a stealth 20. Strength good enough that both passed Entangle. Rolled very high on initiative.

The stench: standing next to two of them forces a Constitution save; on a failure you take 1d4 from the smell alone. Soren's bear nose made it worse. This is probably the save Erlathon passed in Session 0. Aurelian's Shield also kept it out (DM ruling).

Weakness: radiant damage. Jotham's Divine Smite disintegrated one outright, and a second smite left another "falling apart." Masato's fire (Scorching Ray) also disintegrated the dire wolf. Elementalism fire on the tree drew them out but did not obviously hurt them.

Session 0: 1 killed by Jotham.
Session 1: The rest destroyed: Solara 2, Masato 1, plus the dire wolf (Masato). Raised by necromancy, per the party's read of the rotted tree. Who raised them is unknown.
The huge wolf

Lessons and tactics

Things the DM said out loud or that the fight made obvious.

  • Radiant kills the undead. Jotham's smites are the party's win condition. Anyone with radiant or similar options should look for them as the season goes on. Set Jotham up; do not scatter.
  • Fight as a team. The DM's stated design goal. "There's no more 'I'm getting this one, I'm getting this one.' Use your strengths and protect your weaknesses."
  • Do not split off. Aurelian's solo scouting got him boxed in next to the biggest threat. Stealth 20 was not enough against these wolves.
  • Prepare before travel. The DM asked what the party wanted to do before boarding; nobody restocked and now there are no potions. Buy supplies before every expedition.
  • Share kits. Nobody knew who had what. The Spells/potions section of each session now tracks what everyone actually used.
  • Do not waste resources on what you cannot see. When the dire wolf hid, the table's advice was to hold spells until someone spotted it. Bait it out instead: Aurelian's Mage Hand plus a readied shot worked.
  • Ready an action. The DM's explanation: spend your turn to set a trigger ("if something comes out of the forest, I fire"), and it goes off automatically when the trigger happens. Risky, because whatever comes out, friend or squirrel, gets the shot.
  • Long rests take planning. Eight hours in the open is a risk. Pick a defensible spot (the riverbank covered one side) and set a watch.
  • Rules reminders from the DM (2024 rules, played stricter this season):
    • Ranged attack with an enemy within 5 ft is at disadvantage.
    • Hiding in combat is an action, so you cannot Disengage (via Cunning Action) and Hide in the same turn without a second bonus action.
    • Moving away without Disengage provokes an opportunity attack. Disengage is an action (Cunning Action makes it a bonus action for rogues).
    • If you cast a leveled spell as your action, your bonus action spell that turn must be a cantrip.
    • Entangle: save is only on cast. Failures are restrained; successes still leave the 20 ft square as difficult terrain. Half in the cube counts.
    • Shield of Faith must target yourself explicitly in Foundry or it lands on the enemy.
    • Divine Smite is a bonus action spell in the 2024 rules (the DM confirmed it in Session 1), so it shares the turn's bonus action with Shield of Faith.
    • Faerie Fire takes an action.
    • Each Scorching Ray is its own attack roll.
    • Dreadful Strikes only adds to weapon hits, not to spells like Hail of Thorns.
    • Favored Enemy: Erlathon gets two free Hunter's Mark casts per long rest. Uncheck "consume spell slot" in Foundry for those two and keep track yourself.
    • Perception checks and moving are not actions.
    • Wolves get advantage on attacks when a packmate is nearby.
  • Slots are scarce, and Foundry counts them. Jotham ran out mid-fight because Foundry logged Shield of Faith twice. Click spells once, check your slots before your turn, and spend big resources on the biggest threat.
